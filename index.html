From brute force to 6k ± 1

              
    
                
  1. Define the edge cases. 0 and 1 are not prime. 2 and 3 are prime.
    2. 
                
  2. Stop on the first divisor. Once n % d equals zero, n is composite.
    3. 
                
  3. Use the square-root bound. A composite n has at least one factor at most √n.
    4. 
                
  4. Skip known multiples. Any prime greater than 3 must be 6k − 1 or 6k + 1.

  5. Keep checking. 25 and 49 also have those forms but are not prime.

CONCEPT DICTIONARY · SEED
Words behind the code

          

            
Prime number
An integer greater than 1 whose positive divisors are exactly 1 and itself.

            
Modulo / remainder
n % d returns the remainder after integer division. Zero means d divides n exactly.

            
Loop invariant
A statement that remains true at the relevant point of each iteration, helping to prove correctness.

            
Time and space complexity
Asymptotic bounds on operations and extra memory. Fewer checks can improve speed without changing the Big-O class.
